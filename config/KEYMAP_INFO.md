```
  Q(      W           F           P           B           J           L           U           Y           ;
  A       R(CTRL)     S(ALT)      T(SHIFT)    G           M           N(SHIFT)    E(ALT)      I(CTRL)     O
  Z       X           C           D           V           K           H           , . /
                      ESC(ALT)    ENTER(CMD)  SPACE→L1    '(ALT)
```

## Special Keys

- **Homerow Mods**: R, S, T, N, E, I (timeless mods like SM_TD)
- **Layer Taps**: Space (→ Layer 1), Backspace, Delete, Escape (mod-taps)
- **Combos**: Q+W = Tab
- **Macros**: Fun Layer G = Triple Shift

## Timing

- Tapping term: 280ms
- Quick tap: 175ms
- Combo timeout: 50ms
